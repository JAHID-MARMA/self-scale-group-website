About Us

Many skills. One promise: careful work.

This is the Founder’s original Self Scale Academy logo. We keep it just as he gave it.

Self Scale Group is a founder-led, AI-native company. KSM JAHID sets the direction. Our Group CEO and department leads plan the work, do it carefully, check it, and send it out properly. We began with education, where trust matters most, and now also help with documents, media, software and marketing.

Nothing leaves our desk until a person has checked it. If we have not done something, or cannot prove it, we will not claim it. If we cannot do a job well, we will not sell it.

Services

How we can help

Education & Exam Services

We prepare question papers, model tests and exam packages that follow the syllabus, are ready for marking, and arrive when promised.

Teaching Content & Courses

We turn real classroom lessons into clear slides, practice books, spoken-English and phonics lessons, and simple practice software children can use.

Office Documents & Data

Reports, mark sheets, spreadsheets with formulas that add up, and slides that are ready to present and print cleanly.

Design, Social Media & Video

Posters, book covers, thumbnails, posts and videos made with care — clear words, clean sound, music where it helps, and captions people can follow.

Apps & Software

We build small tools that solve real problems for teachers and businesses, then test them properly before anyone relies on them.

Marketing

We write offers, ad copy, captions and simple next steps for courses, schools and local businesses. No fake claims and no pressure tricks — just clear words people can trust.
