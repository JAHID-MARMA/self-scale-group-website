At the moment, payment is by bKash. The steps are simple, and a person checks each payment.

    Make your account

    Sign up or log in so your product has a safe place to wait.

  2. 2
    Choose a product

  3. 3

Questions

Before you buy

How does payment work?

You pay by bKash. After paying, send us the Trx ID and the mobile number you paid from in the shop. We check the payment by hand. Card payment is not open yet.

When does my download open?

It opens after we confirm your payment. Then the product appears in My Products with a private download link. If the link runs out, press Download again to make a new one.

What if the file is wrong or broken?

Our policy is simple: “If the file is wrong or broken we fix or refund within 7 days.” Tell us through the contact form and keep your order details.

Who made the products?

KSM JAHID makes the learning products from his teaching. The team helps prepare the files, and a person checks the final version before it is sold.
