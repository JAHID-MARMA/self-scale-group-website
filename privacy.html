Privacy

Privacy Policy

Plain words, no fine print tricks. This page says what we collect on this website, what we never collect, and how you can reach us about your details. Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

Self Scale Group is a founder-led company based in Jamalpur Sadar, Jamalpur, Bangladesh, founded by Muhammad Jahid Hasan (KSM JAHID). If you have any question about this policy, email the Founder & Chairman at jahid.chairman.ssg@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

What we collect, and why

Contact form. When you send us a message, we keep your name, your email address and your message, so we can read it and reply to you.

Your account. When you sign up or log in, we keep your email address so the site can show you the products you bought. Your password is handled by our secure sign-in service — we do not see it or store it ourselves.

Orders. When you buy a product, we keep the product you chose, the bKash Trx ID and the mobile number you paid from, so we can check your payment and open your download.

Downloads. Product files are given only to the person who bought them, through a private link made for that buyer’s account.

What we never collect

We never ask for your card number, card PIN or mobile banking PIN on this website. Card payment is not open yet; for now, payment is a bKash transfer that you send yourself from your own bKash. We do not sell your personal details to anyone, and this website does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own.

How your details are kept

Your details are stored with the services that run this website: our database (Supabase) and our web hosting (Netlify). Only our small team can see them, and only to do the work you asked for — replying to you, checking a payment, or opening a download. A separate private page that only the Founder can open shows company totals; it never shows any buyer’s rows or details.

Your choices

Your details belong to you. Email us at jahid.chairman.ssg@gmail.com any time to ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it. We will do it and confirm by email.

Buying from us

Short terms

The price shown in the shop is the price you pay. If a product is still being made, no price is shown and it cannot be bought.

When your payment is confirmed, your product waits in My Products under your account. The download link is private and made only for you — please keep it to yourself.

If the file is wrong or broken we fix or refund within 7 days. Tell us through the contact form and keep your order details.

Our learning products are written by KSM JAHID from his own teaching, and a person checks each one before it is sold.
